# Healthcare Actuarial Risk & Insurance Billing Dashboard

## 📊 Executive Summary
This project delivers a comprehensive, data-driven analytical solution designed to evaluate actuarial financial risks, optimize insurance billing workflows, and identify operational cost-containment opportunities within a healthcare environment. By analyzing a dataset of **1,338 patient clinical and financial records**, this interactive dashboard translates complex demographic patterns, health risk factors, and historical billing claims into clear, actionable business intelligence for healthcare executives and operational managers.

The primary objective is to bridge the gap between patient risk profiles and financial outcomes, enabling data-backed decision-making to mitigate high-cost claims and streamline reimbursement cycles.

---

## 🚀 Key Business Insights & Impact
* **Actuarial Risk Stratification:** Segmented population profiles by critical risk drivers (Age, BMI, Smoking Status, and Regional Demographics) to isolate the core variables driving extreme financial liabilities.
* **Financial Leakage Identification:** Uncovered disproportionate billing distributions across medical risk categories, highlighting specific patient segments contributing to peak operational claims.
* **Operational Efficiency:** Provided financial stakeholders with a slice-and-dice interactive interface to rapidly audit average historical charges, cutting down manual reporting loops from days to seconds.

## 📈 Dashboard Architecture & Features
The resulting interactive dashboard provides a comprehensive three-tiered analytical view:

### 1. Actuarial Risk Profiling
* **Impact Metrics:** Aggregates total financial charges across isolated risk cohorts to visualize localized financial exposure.
* **Demographic Breakdown:** Charts consumer distributions geographically and by age intervals to pinpoint macro-level risk trends.

### 2. Behavioral Risk Correlation
* **Behavior Matrix:** Directly isolates the financial impact of high-risk habits (e.g., lifestyle choices, smoking status) against a baseline population.
* **BMI Analysis:** Cross-references body mass index brackets against clinical costs to identify critical preventative health intervention windows.

### 3. Financial & Claims Analysis
* **Charge Distribution:** Displays granular billing breakdowns, allowing financial teams to explore average vs. peak distribution ranges.
* **Operational Auditing Filter:** Enables dynamic filtering by clinical traits, allowing medical coders and compliance teams to rapidly audit historical trends.
